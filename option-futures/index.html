Combined rule

Option vote = ORB15+CPR: CE when spot is above the 09:15-09:30 high AND CPR TC, PE when below the low AND CPR BC, else flat.
Futures vote = MTF score (+1/-1 per 3/5/15/60-min bar by price direction) plus fresh OI build-up (LONG BUILDUP / SHORT BUILDUP bars).
BUY CALL = option vote CE and futures confirm (score > 0 or any LONG BUILDUP). BUY PUT = mirror. Futures against = NO TRADE (conflict). Option flat = NO TRADE. Exit when the option vote flips the other way, or 15:25.

Research, 58 sessions 26 Aug - 19 Nov 2026 (NIFTY points, 4 pts cost): ORB15+CPR alone +951 pts PF 1.40; with OI build-up confirm +1210 pts PF 1.60, 62% win, 56 trades. Not statistically significant (t 1.34), and 26 Aug - 7 Oct was negative. A lean, not a proven edge.
